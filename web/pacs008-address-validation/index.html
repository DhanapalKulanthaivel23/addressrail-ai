pacs.008 debtor and creditor address validation

The pacs.008 FI-to-FI customer credit transfer message can contain debtor and creditor parties with postal-address elements. Missing town or country values may require review depending on the applicable message profile, rule version and business scenario.

Payment-message address examples

Synthetic debtor address requiring review

<Dbtr>
  <Nm>SAMPLE COMPANY</Nm>
  <PstlAdr>
    <AdrLine>10 High Street, London, GB</AdrLine>
  </PstlAdr>
</Dbtr>

Illustrative structured fields for review

<Dbtr>
  <Nm>SAMPLE COMPANY</Nm>
  <PstlAdr>
    <StrtNm>High Street</StrtNm>
    <BldgNb>10</BldgNb>
    <TwnNm>London</TwnNm>
    <Ctry>GB</Ctry>
  </PstlAdr>
</Dbtr>

The second example is fully structured. When a hybrid form is permitted, any remaining AdrLine follows structured TwnNm and Ctry in the postal-address element sequence. Do not place AdrLine before TwnNm in the same PstlAdr. Schema and scheme-profile validation are not performed.

What to check

  • The XML tag PstlAdr identifies a postal-address container; AdrLine does not by itself supply structured TwnNm and Ctry values.
  • AddressRail AI detects some missing town/country fields and produces review-only proposals from simple comma-separated input patterns.
  • When no reliable source evidence exists, the correct outcome is an unresolved issue—not an invented postal detail.
  • Official scheme conformance and message-profile validation need authoritative rule versions and additional fields beyond the current prototype.

Rule versions and current address guidance

The EPC 2025 SCT, SCT Inst and related scheme rulebooks v1.2 effective 30 September 2026 postponed the previously planned 15 November 2026 end of unstructured postal addresses. A new end date is not asserted here. EPC rules are separate from SWIFT CBPR+ usage guidelines and domestic scheme profiles.

Address examples and message fragments are incomplete learning sketches, not official certified bank messages. Always confirm the current release, namespaces, business rules, participant eligibility and source documentation.
