pacs.008 debtor and creditor address validation

The pacs.008 FI-to-FI customer credit transfer message can contain debtor and creditor parties with postal-address elements. Missing town or country values may require review depending on the applicable message profile, rule version and business scenario.

What to check

  • The XML tag PstlAdr identifies a postal-address container; AdrLine does not by itself supply structured TwnNm and Ctry values.
  • AddressRail AI detects some missing town/country fields and produces review-only proposals from simple comma-separated input patterns.
  • When no reliable source evidence exists, the correct outcome is an unresolved issue—not an invented postal detail.
  • Official scheme conformance and message-profile validation need authoritative rule versions and additional fields beyond the current prototype.
