02 / CHAPS OPERATIONS
Follow the settlement boundary
  1. Originating bank receives and authorises a high-value GBP payment.
  2. CHAPS participant carries out its own controls and prepares the applicable ISO 20022 message.
  3. CHAPS/RTGS processes payments individually on a gross basis, subject to settlement liquidity and operating rules.
  4. When authoritative RTGS evidence confirms settlement, that is distinct from beneficiary-account posting or an AI-generated explanation.
  5. Insufficient liquidity may delay progress; missing confirmation produces an uncertain state that requires reconciliation.
