03 / LEARN THE DISTINCTIONS

One payment. Different messages.

pain.001

A customer-to-bank credit transfer initiation. Its presence does not establish that the bank accepted or processed the payment.

pacs.008

An interbank customer credit transfer. It represents messaging between financial institutions—not proof of settlement or beneficiary credit.

pain.002 / pacs.002

Customer-facing and FI-to-FI status reporting have different contexts. A local bank validation rejection is not a fictional response from STEP2.

STEP2 SCT processes transfers using a continuous gross settlement model. These browser events are fictional learning steps and contain no official clearing responses.
