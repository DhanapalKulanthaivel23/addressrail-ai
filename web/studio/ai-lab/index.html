LOCAL-ONLY REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

What an evidence-grounded payment agent would need.

Local-only design: an LLM can explain evidence or propose a next review, but cannot execute production payment actions. A validation failure or absent evidence routes to human investigation—not settlement.

REGULATORY CONTEXT / CHECK CURRENT TEXT

Governance responsibilities before real deployment.

Human review: keep authorised banking controls outside an LLM; log reviewer identity, supplied evidence, decisions and model version if an actual implementation is deployed.

Data minimisation: avoid raw customer/payment details in public AI trials. Define retention, access controls and bank legal basis before processing regulated or personal information.

UK: consider UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 plus applicable FCA/PRA operational resilience and outsourcing requirements. Their applicability depends on the legal entity and activity.

EU: consider GDPR, the EU AI Act (phased obligations) and DORA for financial entities within scope. This is not a claim that every payment-explanation model is a high-risk AI system.
