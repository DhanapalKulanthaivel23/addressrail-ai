SWIFT MT103 address correction

Swift migration milestone: The CBPR+ MT/ISO 20022 coexistence period for cross-border interbank payment instructions ended on 22 November 2025. This did not remove all possible MT103 uses: limited contingency translation remains subject to Swift restrictions, and other services may have different rules. Swift's migration presentation ↗.

Legacy / transition example: MT103 illustrates traditional FIN customer-payment fields, not the default format for new cross-border CBPR+ MX instructions. Network and service eligibility vary by Swift release. MT103 customer fields may contain customer names and postal addresses as free-text lines or formatted components. A payment address tool can identify fields that need review before mapping a message to ISO 20022. This does not itself convert an MT103 payment to pacs.008.

What to check

  • Field :50a: generally identifies the ordering customer; :59a: identifies the beneficiary customer. Specific option letters affect permitted formats and interpretation.
  • Free-text address lines can contain place names, building data, postcode and country in different orders; extraction must not invent missing information.
  • Check current Swift network/service rules before assuming MT103 remains eligible for a specific production payment path.
  • Converting MT103 to pacs.008 requires many additional message-field mappings and scheme-specific checks beyond postal-address structuring.
  • The current AddressRail AI demo performs limited customer-field and address-pattern inspection. It is not a certified MT parser or an end-to-end message converter.

Rule versions and current address guidance

The EPC 2025 SCT, SCT Inst and related scheme rulebooks v1.2 effective 30 September 2026 postponed the previously planned 15 November 2026 end of unstructured postal addresses. A new end date is not asserted here. EPC rules are separate from SWIFT CBPR+ usage guidelines and domestic scheme profiles.

Address examples and message fragments are incomplete learning sketches, not official certified bank messages. Always confirm the current release, namespaces, business rules, participant eligibility and source documentation.
