ISO 20022 structured and hybrid addresses

ISO 20022 offers named XML elements for parts of a postal address. But an MX message can still carry unstructured information in AdrLine. AddressRail AI explores address-quality checks while future releases target more complete mapping and validation.

Payment-message address examples

Unstructured postal address

<PstlAdr>
  <AdrLine>10 High Street, London, GB</AdrLine>
</PstlAdr>

Structured postal address

<PstlAdr>
  <StrtNm>High Street</StrtNm>
  <BldgNb>10</BldgNb>
  <TwnNm>London</TwnNm>
  <Ctry>GB</Ctry>
</PstlAdr>

Hybrid-style teaching example (profile dependent)

<PstlAdr>
  <TwnNm>London</TwnNm>
  <Ctry>GB</Ctry>
  <AdrLine>10 High Street</AdrLine>
</PstlAdr>

The order matters: TwnNm and Ctry precede AdrLine in this example. Actual usage depends on message version, XSD, market-practice rules and implementation guidelines.

What to check

  • Nm is the party name; PstlAdr holds postal-address details. TwnNm and Ctry represent town and country values.
  • A hybrid address combines designated structured elements with remaining address information in AdrLine where the applicable message/profile permits it.
  • The presence of XML elements alone does not prove that field contents are accurate, syntactically valid or accepted by a particular payment scheme.
  • The current browser demo checks for town/country elements and suggests limited human-reviewed mappings. It does not enforce all ISO 20022 or CBPR+ business rules.

Rule versions and current address guidance

The EPC 2025 SCT, SCT Inst and related scheme rulebooks v1.2 effective 30 September 2026 postponed the previously planned 15 November 2026 end of unstructured postal addresses. A new end date is not asserted here. EPC rules are separate from SWIFT CBPR+ usage guidelines and domestic scheme profiles.

Address examples and message fragments are incomplete learning sketches, not official certified bank messages. Always confirm the current release, namespaces, business rules, participant eligibility and source documentation.
