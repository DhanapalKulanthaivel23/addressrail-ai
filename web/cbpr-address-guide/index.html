CBPR+ structured and hybrid postal-address guide

CBPR+ is a payment-market practice for cross-border ISO 20022 messaging. Postal-address requirements can vary by implementation guideline and release. The previously announced EPC end date of 15 November 2026 was postponed in September 2026. EPC address rules are distinct from Swift CBPR+ message-profile requirements; this guide does not claim a universal Swift cutover date.

What to check

  • Whether an address format is accepted depends on the exact message definition, applicable scheme profile and effective rule release.
  • Do not assume a single address migration deadline applies to all schemes or future CBPR+ releases.
  • Validate both technical XML constraints and market-practice/business rules against the current official documentation.
  • AddressRail AI currently performs limited address-quality inspection, not certified CBPR+ validation or automatic message repair.

Rule versions and current address guidance

The EPC 2025 SCT, SCT Inst and related scheme rulebooks v1.2 effective 30 September 2026 postponed the previously planned 15 November 2026 end of unstructured postal addresses. A new end date is not asserted here. EPC rules are separate from SWIFT CBPR+ usage guidelines and domestic scheme profiles.

Address examples and message fragments are incomplete learning sketches, not official certified bank messages. Always confirm the current release, namespaces, business rules, participant eligibility and source documentation.
