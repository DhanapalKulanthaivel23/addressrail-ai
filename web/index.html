HOW ADDRESSRAIL AI WORKS

Address quality → AI explanation → human review

Our existing address analyzer combines deterministic checks with optional Gemini commentary. It does not treat AI output as validation or automatically change payment messages.

01 / Parse & inspect

Load synthetic MT103 or pacs.008 and identify the address parties.

02 / Apply rules

Detect missing structured town/country fields and review-only repair candidates supported by original text.

03 / Optional Gemini

Use the existing protected AI service for an explanation, only with synthetic or anonymised input and consent.

04 / Human decision

Compare original and proposed addresses. No repair is approved or applied automatically.
